Frequently asked questions

Where do the papers come from?

Eratosthenes searches OpenAlex, the open index of scholarly works, and arXiv. Journal articles, conference papers and preprints are all included.

How is this different from a keyword search?

A keyword search returns papers that share your words. Eratosthenes scores how closely the meaning of each paper matches your question, so a relevant paper that uses different terms still ranks near the top.

What is the difference between quick search and deep search?

Quick search ranks up to 60 of the most relevant papers for your query. Deep search reads up to 200, which matters for broad questions and cross-disciplinary topics.

What is a Research-Gap Graph?

It maps the papers in your search as a landscape: topics that are well studied form deep basins, and the barriers between them are the open gaps. Each paper gets an Impact score for how much of a gap it closes.

Is Eratosthenes free?

Yes. Quick search and topic maps are unlimited and free forever, with 3 deep searches and 3 Research-Gap Graphs a month. Pro, Max and Team plans raise those limits; see the pricing page.

Are my searches saved?

Yes. Every search is saved to your account, so you can reopen any past session from the sidebar and export its citations.

Do Eratosthenes and Benjamin share a subscription?

No. You sign in to both with the same SciCoagent account, but each product has its own plan, so you only pay for what you use.
