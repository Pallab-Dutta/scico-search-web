Questions

What does the free plan include?

Unlimited quick search and topic maps, free forever, plus 3 deep searches and 3 Research-Gap Graphs a month. The author and PI finder, saved sessions and citation export are included too.

What is the difference between quick search and deep search?

Quick search ranks up to 60 of the most relevant papers for your query. Deep search reads up to 200, which matters for broad questions and cross-disciplinary topics.

What is a Research-Gap Graph?

It maps the papers in your search as a landscape: topics that are well studied form deep basins, and the barriers between them are the open gaps. Each paper gets an Impact score for how much of a gap it closes. Opening a graph you already built does not count again.

When do my limits reset?

On the 1st of each month (UTC). Max is unlimited under fair use, up to 300 deep searches and 300 Research-Gap Graphs a month.

Can I buy plans for my lab or institution?

Yes. Team plans start at 3 seats, with everything in Pro for every member and one invoice for the group. For a department or institution, get in touch.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for and is not renewed after that.

Do Eratosthenes and Benjamin share a subscription?

No. You sign in to both with the same SciCoagent account, but each product has its own plan, so you only pay for what you use.
